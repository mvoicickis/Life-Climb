Mountain stages — ledge mockup

360px frames side by side. Frames 1–6 are the original map scenarios. M1–M2 show the open-ledge carousel in detail. A1–A5 show Arrange camps (opened from the HUD button). Every button is a command — no confirmation dialogs.

Open ledge rules

  • 1 camp — centered on the ledge
  • 2 camps — side by side, names wrap to 2 lines, never cut off
  • 3+ camps — 2 visible, ‹ › arrows (44px), dots under the ledge (one per camp); current camp = green outline name pill; bottom battle card stays fixed while paging
  • Only the open ledge gets arrows — fog and done ledges stay as now
  • Map box — 940:1672 image at full width, no crop; bottom terrace ≈10–90% wide; Frame 0 shows L/centre/R dots per terrace
  • Terrace order — bottom=open, second=fog, third=fog, top=cloud; done badge centred on stairs below bottom terrace

Arrange camps

  • Highest stage at top, stage 1 at bottom — mountain-shaped list
  • Tap a camp to lift it (green); every stage shows a Move here spot; ↑ ↓ reorder within a stage
  • + Add stage between every pair and at the top; stage menu → Delete stage (camps drop to the stage below)
  • Stages with 4+ camps fold to one line — tap to expand
