Camp view — frosted glass over camp fire

360px click-through. Tick a battle to slide it out and show a 5s undo toast. Won today is a fixed bottom strip; tap it to slide the done list up.

How to use

  • Tap + Add battle to expand the composer. The input focuses automatically.
  • Type a battle, then tap the green + or press Enter to save. Focus stays for the next one.
  • Tap away with an empty input to collapse back to the quiet add row.
  • Tick an open card to slide it out. Tap Undo on the toast within 5 seconds, or open Won today at the bottom.
