Camp fire — travelling base camp

Static mockup at 360px. Base camp is no longer fixed at the bottom of the mountain. It is a fire that travels with the climber and burns beside whichever camp they are on.

Rules

  • Beside the current camp marker: tent (picture only), fire (only tappable thing here), fox lying by the fire.
  • Tap the fire: sheet with daily things and side battles that belong to no camp. Add row always visible at the bottom.
  • Fire burns brighter the more days in a row you show up. No numbers. No shame. Never goes out.
  • When a camp is finished, the fire leaves. Nothing stays behind at old camps.
  • New players: the fire stays hidden until they win their first battle. They see only the camp marker — one obvious thing to tap.
