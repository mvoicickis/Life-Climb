LifePoints

A simple story about our app — written so anyone can understand it.

What is LifePoints?

LifePoints is like a sticker chart for grown-up goals. You pick good habits. When you do them, you get points. More points = you did more good things!

1. The big idea

Imagine three jars on a shelf:

Jar 1: You

Your account. Your email. Your password. Your points.

Jar 2: Habits

Things you want to do, like “Run” or “Read”.

Jar 3: Completions

A note that says “I did this habit today!” and how many points you earned.

When you press Complete, the app puts a note in Jar 3 and adds points to Jar 1.

2. How you use it (step by step)

  1. Start the app on your computer with bin/dev.
  2. Open the website (usually http://localhost:3000).
  3. Sign up (new account) or Sign in (already have one).
  4. Go to Habits and make a habit (example: “Morning run”, 10 points).
  5. Go to the Dashboard.
  6. Press Complete when you finish that habit today.
  7. Watch your points go up!

3. What each screen does

Dashboard

  • Total points — all points you ever earned
  • Points today — points you earned just today
  • Today’s habits — habits you can complete
  • Recent activity — the last things you finished

Habits page

  • Make a new habit
  • Edit a habit
  • Delete a habit
  • Turn a habit on/off (active)

4. The rules (important!)

  • You can complete each habit only once per day.
  • Points must be a number bigger than 0.
  • A habit needs a name.
  • Only you can see your habits (your account keeps them private).

5. What’s inside the code (simple map)

Think of the app like a restaurant:

  • Views = the menus you see (HTML pages)
  • Controllers = the waiters (they take your click and do something)
  • Models = the kitchen recipes (User, Habit, Completion)
  • Database = the fridge (stores your data in SQLite)

Main folders

  • app/models/ — User, Habit, Completion
  • app/controllers/ — Dashboard, Habits, Completions, Sessions
  • app/views/ — the pages you look at
  • db/migrate/ — instructions that built the database tables
  • db/seeds.rb — demo user + sample habits

6. What we built for the MVP

  • Sign up / Sign in / Sign out
  • Create, edit, and delete habits
  • Complete a habit and earn points
  • Dashboard with totals and recent activity
  • Live update when you complete (no full page reload)
  • Tests to make sure the happy path works

7. What we did NOT build yet

(These can come later)

  • Streaks (days in a row)
  • Charts and graphs
  • Email reminders
  • Phone app install (PWA)
  • Leaderboards / friends
